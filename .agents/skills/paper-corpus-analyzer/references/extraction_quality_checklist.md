# Extraction Quality Checklist

- The output is not a literature review.
- Each rule is transferable to paper outlining or manuscript writing.
- Each rule has a source `paper_id` when it comes from a paper.
- Unknown journal, venue, domain, or paper type is marked `unknown`.
- Long original passages are not copied.
- DOI, venue, figures, experiments, and conclusions are not fabricated.
- The extraction distinguishes paper-specific observations from general advice.
- Problem formulation, method, experiment, figure/table, writing, and reviewer-risk patterns are all checked.
- Claim-control observations are included for writing extracts.
