# Results Discussion Writing Template

Status: pre-corpus template. Replace TODO source IDs after synthesis.

## Results paragraph moves

1. State the experiment or comparison.
2. Name the metric and scenario.
3. Report the key pattern or numerical finding.
4. Interpret why the pattern matters.
5. Connect the result to a contribution.
6. Mention uncertainty, failure cases, or boundary conditions when visible.

## Discussion paragraph moves

1. Synthesize findings across experiments.
2. Explain practical or theoretical implication.
3. Identify limitations.
4. State future work without overstating certainty.

## Risk checks

- Do not generalize beyond tested scenarios.
- Do not claim causality from correlation or comparison alone.
