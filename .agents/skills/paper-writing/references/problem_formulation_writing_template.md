# Problem Formulation Writing Template

Status: pre-corpus template. Replace TODO source IDs after synthesis.

## Writing order

1. Start with a scenario paragraph.
2. State the decision, prediction, estimation, assessment, or design problem.
3. Introduce assumptions and scope boundaries.
4. Define notation in groups.
5. Explain variables, objectives, and constraints in prose before or around equations.
6. After equations, map major terms back to real-world meaning.
7. End by connecting the formulation to the method section.

## Risk checks

- Do not open the section with unexplained equations.
- Do not hide important assumptions in notation.
- Do not use symbols before definition.
