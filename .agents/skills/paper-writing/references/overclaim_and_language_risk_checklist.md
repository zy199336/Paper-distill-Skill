# Overclaim And Language Risk Checklist

Status: pre-corpus template. Replace TODO source IDs after synthesis.

## Overclaim signals

- "first", "universal", "optimal", "always", "fully solves", or "proves" without strict evidence.
- Practical implications stated beyond the experiment setting.
- Novelty stated without comparison to prior work.
- Performance claims without baselines or metrics.
- Robustness claims without sensitivity or stress tests.

## Safer alternatives

- "is designed to"
- "can support"
- "shows potential for"
- "within the tested scenarios"
- "the results suggest"
- "compared with the selected baselines"

## Required flags

- Missing citations
- Missing metrics
- Missing parameter settings
- Missing assumptions
- Missing limitations
